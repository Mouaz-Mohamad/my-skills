# video-storyboard

## Purpose
Turn a scene plan into a production-ready storyboard and matching per-shot generation or filming prompts with continuity constraints.

## Use When
- Editing or preparing video content for Dart, ads, Reels, Shorts, tutorials, or product storytelling.

## Video Workflow
1. Inspect source dimensions, duration, frame rate, audio, and transcript before editing.
2. Define the communication goal and the moments that prove or advance it.
3. Create an edit/overlay/storyboard plan before destructive rendering.
4. Preserve brand/product continuity and avoid inventing claims or product details.
5. Verify final timing, safe areas, captions, audio intelligibility, and mobile framing.
6. Export a delivery version plus the edit plan or evidence used to make decisions.

## Dart Creative Rules
- Keep product visibility and brand recognition deliberate, not accidental.
- Do not overload short videos with text; prioritize one message per beat.
- For paid ads, preserve a clear hook, product proof, offer/context, and CTA where the brief calls for them.
- Check vertical crops on real mobile-safe areas.

## Focus Areas
- storyboard
- shot list
- continuity
- video prompts
- timing

## Output Checklist
- Scope is explicit.
- Assumptions are labeled and unverifiable facts are not invented.
- The user gets an actionable next step or deliverable.
- Edge cases and failure modes relevant to the task were considered.
- The output can be checked against evidence or a clear success criterion.
